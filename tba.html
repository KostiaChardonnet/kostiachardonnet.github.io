Workshop TBA 2026

on Taylor, Böhm and Approximations.

The first TBA Workshop will take place in Paris, at the Inria Center, on the 16th of November 2026. The goal of this workshop is to stir collaborations in the community of the theory of approximation of the lambda-calculus.

Date: Monday, 16 of November 2026

Talks · Schedule · Registration · Venue · Organisers

Talks

  • Davide Barbarossa: Thoughts around the notion of approximation.
  • Jules Chouquet: Meaningfulness VS non-trivial approximation in distant Bang calculus.
  • Thomas Ehrhard: Coherent differentiation: taming the sums of differential linear logic.
  • Giulio Guerrieri: Meaningfulness and Genericity in a Subsuming Framework.
  • Adrienne Lancelot: Semantically Free Variables and why Böhm should care.
  • Giulio Manzonetto: What do I do if I have time?
  • Damiano Mazza: Approximations dans le pi-calcul.
  • Federico Olimpieri: Combinatorial species and the approximation theories of lambda-terms.
